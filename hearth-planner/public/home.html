Questions, answered

Getting to know GuidED.

Who is GuidED designed for?

GuidED is designed to support teachers, parents and learners, with tools for lesson preparation, teaching resources and learning support at home.

Does GuidED follow Ghana’s curriculum?

GuidED uses available curriculum structures and selections to guide lesson preparation. Teachers should review generated materials and align them with the specific needs of their class.

Does Super Intelligence replace a teacher?

No. GuidED is a teaching and learning companion. Educators remain responsible for reviewing, adapting and delivering learning experiences.

How do I get started?

Open GuidED, create or access your account, and explore the tools available for your role.
