GES Promotion Requirements: How to Read the Scheme and Prepare

The GES Scheme of Service sets out progression conditions by grade and role. It does not say that years served alone guarantee promotion. Requirements differ across grades and positions, so use the exact profile that applies to the route you are considering.

Common conditions shown in grade profiles

Several professional-class profiles in the Schools & Instructions Division specify a minimum of four years in the current grade, satisfactory Staff Performance Appraisal Reports for three consecutive years with an average score of at least 70%, and a GES aptitude test. Some transitions also require a selection interview. These are examples from the profiles, not a universal rule for every GES grade or staff category (printed pages 369–373).

Examples from the Schools & Instructions profiles

  • Assistant Director II to Assistant Director I: the profile specifies at least four years as Assistant Director II, three consecutive years of satisfactory appraisal averaging 70% or above, and a GES aptitude test (printed page 373).
  • Assistant Director I to Deputy Director: the profile specifies at least four years as Assistant Director I, the same three-year appraisal threshold, a GES aptitude test and a selection interview conducted by the GES Council in collaboration with the Public Services Commission (printed pages 371–372).
  • Deputy Director to Director II: the profile specifies at least four years as Deputy Director, a relevant master’s degree, three consecutive years of satisfactory appraisal averaging 70% or above, a GES aptitude test and a selection interview (printed page 370).
  • Director II to Director I: the profile specifies at least four years as Director II, the three-year appraisal threshold, a GES aptitude test and a selection interview (printed pages 366–368).

Aptitude tests and selection interviews are not the same

The Scheme’s summary table distinguishes aptitude tests from selection interviews and shows that the requirements vary by grade and route (printed page 27). Some grades or analogous grades have an aptitude-test requirement; some require a selection interview; some routes list both; and some entries indicate that a test or interview is not required. Always check the exact grade profile and the summary table rather than assuming one process applies to everyone.

Build a promotion-readiness file

  • Appointment and confirmation letters, plus any promotion or regrading letters.
  • Staff Performance Appraisal Reports for the relevant consecutive years.
  • Certificates, transcripts and evidence of professional or academic qualifications.
  • Records of relevant duties, leadership responsibilities, projects and measurable contributions.
  • Evidence of professional learning, training and competencies linked to the target grade.
  • Any official circular, vacancy notice, application instruction or communication relevant to the exercise.

Questions to confirm with HR before applying

  • Which version of the Scheme and which current circular govern this exercise?
  • What is the exact grade or analogous grade from which I am applying?
  • How is the required time on grade calculated for this exercise?
  • Which appraisal years and minimum average score will be accepted?
  • Is an aptitude test, selection interview, or both required?
  • Are there vacancies, additional qualifications or other conditions for this particular route?
  • What documents, deadlines and submission channels apply?

Do not treat this guide as an eligibility ruling

The Scheme copy supplied for this guide has a blank implementation-date field (printed page 17). In addition, individual eligibility depends on verified employment records, the applicable version and current administrative instructions. This guide cannot confirm that a person qualifies, that a promotion is due, or that a particular exercise is open. Obtain confirmation from the appropriate GES HR office before acting.

In short

Prepare by checking the exact grade profile, organising appraisal and qualification evidence, and confirming the current process with HR. Time served by itself does not guarantee promotion.
