About GuidEd

A lesson-planning assistant for Ghana teachers, from Basic 1 to SHS 3. Pick a subject, class and week, and get a ready-to-teach plan in minutes.

What it does

  • Writes two lessons for the week, aligned with the Ghana Education Service (GES) and NaCCA curriculum, for Lower Primary (Basic 1 to 3), Upper Primary (Basic 4 to 6), JHS (Basic 7 to 9) and SHS 1 to 3 (English Language, Mathematics, General Science and Social Studies).
  • Can add notes and exam practice that suit the level: a class exercise in primary, BECE-style questions at JHS and WASSCE-style questions at SHS, each with a marking guide.
  • Lets you ask for changes in plain language, such as a simpler activity or a different example.
  • Exports to Word so you can print it, edit it and put your name and school on it.

Built for the classroom, and for weak signal

GuidEd installs on your phone like an app, and your saved plans stay on your device so you can read them offline. Writing a new plan needs a connection.

Free and Pro

You can try a Lesson 1 preview without an account. A free account gives you full plans each week and keeps them in sync across your phone and computer. Pro removes the weekly limits and the footer on Word files. You pay once for a period of time through Paystack, and nothing renews on its own. See the refund policy.

Please check before you teach

GuidEd uses AI, and AI can make mistakes. Read each plan, check it against your syllabus and textbook, and adjust it for your learners. You are the teacher; GuidEd is the helper. GuidEd is an independent tool and is not an official product of the Ghana Education Service or NaCCA.
