Common questions

Is it free?

Yes, a free account gives you full plans each week. Pro removes the weekly limits and the footer on Word files.

Does it follow the NaCCA curriculum?

Plans follow the schemes of learning for each class and week. Always check a plan against your syllabus and textbook before you teach. GuidEd uses AI and can make mistakes.

Is GuidEd an official GES or NaCCA product?

No. It is an independent tool for teachers.
