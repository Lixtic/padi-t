What Basic 2 English Language covers

Topics by week, from the scheme of learning that GuidEd uses to write each plan.

Term 1

  • Weeks 2–6: 1.1 Songs, Poems, Rhymes and Drama
    B2.1.1.1 Listen to and enjoy rhymes and simple songs
  • Weeks 7–10: 1.2 Listening and Comprehension
    B2.1.2.1 Listen to short audio or stories and respond
  • Weeks 11–12: 2.1 Phonics and Word Knowledge
    B2.2.1.1 Blend sounds to read simple CVC words

Term 2

  • Weeks 2–3: 2.1 Phonics and Word Knowledge
    B2.2.1.1 Blend sounds to read simple CVC words
  • Weeks 4–7: 2.2 Comprehension
    B2.2.2.1 Read and understand short sentences
  • Weeks 8–11: 3.1 Grammar and Usage
    B2.3.1.1 Use simple nouns and pronouns
  • Week 12: 3.2 Punctuation
    B2.3.2.1 Recognize basic punctuation

Term 3

  • Weeks 2–4: 3.2 Punctuation
    B2.3.2.1 Recognize basic punctuation
  • Weeks 5–8: 4.1 Composition
    B2.4.1.1 Write words and short phrases
  • Weeks 9–12: 5.1 Appreciating Literature
    B2.5.1.1 Enjoy listening to folktales
