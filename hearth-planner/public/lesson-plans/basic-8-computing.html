What Basic 8 Computing covers

Topics by week, from the scheme of learning that GuidEd uses to write each plan.

Term 1

  • Weeks 2–4: Introduction to Computing: Components of Computers and Computer Systems
    B8.1.1.1. Identify parts of a computer and technology tools; B8.1.1.2. Demonstrate the use of Desktop features
  • Weeks 5–7: Introduction to Computing: Technology in the Community
    B8.1.2.1. Demonstrate the use of Technology in the Community
  • Weeks 8–9: Introduction to Computing: Health and Safety in Using ICT Tools
    B8.1.3.1. Demonstrate how to apply Health and Safety measures
  • Weeks 10–12: Productivity Software: Introduction to Word Processing
    B8.2.1.1 Demonstrate how to use MS Word (tables & hyperlink pages)

Term 2

  • Weeks 2–4: Productivity Software: Introduction To Presentation
    B8.2.2.1. Demonstrate how to use Microsoft PowerPoint (Multimedia)
  • Weeks 5–10: Productivity Software: Introduction To Desktop Publishing
    B8.2.3.1. Demonstrate how to use MS-Publisher
  • Weeks 11–12: Productivity Software: Introduction To Electronic Spreadsheet
    B8.2.4.1. Demonstrate how to use the Spreadsheet. (using functions and complex formulas)

Term 3

  • Weeks 2–3: Communication Networks: Computer Networks
    B8.3.1.1. Identify the concept of computer networking for global communication
  • Week 4: Communication Networks: Internet And Social Media
    B8.3.2.1. Demonstrate the use of Social Networking and Electronic Mail
  • Weeks 5–6: Communication Networks: Information Security
    B8.3.3.1. Recognise data threats and security protections
  • Weeks 7–8: Communication Networks: Web Technologies
    B8.3.4.1. Demonstrate the use of a web browser (Search engine)
  • Week 9: Computational Thinking: Introduction To Programming
    B8.4.1.1. Show an understanding of the concept of programming
  • Week 10: Computational Thinking: Algorithm
    B8.4.2.1. Analyse the correct step-by-step procedure in solving any real-world problem
  • Week 11: Computational Thinking: Robotics
    B8.4.3.1. Discuss Robot Intelligence Concepts
  • Week 12: Computational Thinking: Artificial Intelligence
    B8.4.4.1. Discuss Artificial Intelligence Concepts
