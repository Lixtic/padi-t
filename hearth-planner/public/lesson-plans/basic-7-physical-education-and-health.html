What Basic 7 Physical Education and Health covers

Topics by week, from the scheme of learning that GuidEd uses to write each plan.

Term 1

  • Weeks 2–4: Health Education: Nutrition And Physical Activity
    B7.1.1.1. Demonstrate understanding of various food nutrients required for sports and physical activity.
  • Weeks 5–6: Health Education: Disease Prevention And Management
    B7.1.2.1. Demonstrate understanding of common diseases associated with sedentary behaviours and physical inactivity
  • Weeks 7–10: Health Education: First Aid, Injury Prevention And Management
    B7.1.3.1. Demonstrate understanding of first aid, causes, prevention and management of common injuries in sports and physical activity.
  • Weeks 11–12: Physical Activity Education: Traditional Rhythmic Gymnastics, Games And Dance
    B7.2.1.1. Demonstrate understanding of the varieties of traditional games and adaptations for inclusivity and cultural identity (e.g., conceptual and cooperative games, etc.)

Term 2

  • Weeks 2–5: Physical Activity Education: Traditional Rhythmic Gymnastics, Games And Dance
    B7.2.1.2. Demonstrate understanding of the varieties of traditional dances and adaptations for inclusivity and cultural identity (e.g., individual and group dances, etc.); B7.2.1.3. Demonstrate understanding and apply skills and movement techniques in global and traditional rhythmic gymnastics and adaptations for global awareness and cultural sensitivity
  • Weeks 6–12: Physical Activity Education: Physical Fitness
    B7.2.2.1. Demonstrate the ability to participate in a variety of internationally benchmarked physical fitness indicators (e.g., muscular strength and endurance, cardiorespiratory strength and endurance, flexibility and balance fitness indicators); B7.2.2.2. Demonstrate the ability to participate in a variety of traditional and global cardiorespiratory activities; B7.2.2.3. Demonstrate the ability to participate in a variety of muscular strength and endurance activities

Term 3

  • Weeks 2–12: Physical Activity Education: Organised Sports And Physical Activity Participation
    B7.2.3.1. Demonstrate the ability to apply movement concepts, principles, and strategies in performing non–contact sports (e.g., ball and racket, athletics, and individual and target sports) to develop creativity, innovation, communication, and collaboration; B7.2.3.2; B7.2.3.3
