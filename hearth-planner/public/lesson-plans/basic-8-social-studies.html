What Basic 8 Social Studies covers

Topics by week, from the scheme of learning that GuidEd uses to write each plan.

Term 2

  • Week 2: Family Life: The Family
    B8.2.2.1. Show understanding of the family and family life issues
  • Week 3: Family Life: Population
    B8.2.4.1. Analyse the population structure in Ghana and its related issues
  • Weeks 4–5: Sense Of Purpose: The Individual And The Community
    B8.3.1.1. ; B8.3.2.1. Demonstrate knowledge of the role of the individual in the community
  • Week 6: Law And Order: Citizenship And Human Rights
    B8.4.1.1. Investigate the rights and responsibilities of a citizen
  • Week 7: Law And Order: Conflict Prevention And Management
    B8.4.2.1. Analyse ways of preventing and managing conflict

Term 3

  • Week 2: Socio-Economic Development: Financial And Investment Issues
    B8.5.2.1. Demonstrate understanding of employer and employee relations in social security and pension
  • Week 3: Socio-Economic Development: Tourism
    B8.5.3.1. Evaluate tourism as an important economic sector for national development
  • Weeks 4–7: Nationhood: The Republics
    B8.6.2.1. Analyse the main developments in the Republics between 1960 and 1972
