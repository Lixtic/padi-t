What Basic 9 Computing covers

Topics by week, from the scheme of learning that GuidEd uses to write each plan.

Term 1

  • Weeks 2–5: Introduction to Computing: Components of Computers and Computer Systems
    B9.1.1.1 Identify parts of a Computer and Technology Tools; B9.1.1.2. Demonstrate the Use of the Desktop
  • Weeks 6–7: Introduction to Computing: Technology in the Community (Communication)
    B9.1.2.1. Demonstrate the Use of Technology in the Community
  • Weeks 8–9: Introduction to Computing: Health and Safety in the Use of ICT Tools
    B9.1.3.1. Demonstrate How to Apply Health and Safety Measures
  • Weeks 10–12: Productivity Software: Introduction to Word Processing
    B9.2.1.1 Demonstrate How to Use MS Word (Multimedia)

Term 2

  • Weeks 2–4: Productivity Software: Introduction To Presentation
    B9.2.2.1. Demonstrate How to use Microsoft PowerPoint (Multimedia)
  • Weeks 5–7: Productivity Software: Introduction To Desktop Publishing
    B9.2.3.1. Critique a Desktop Published Document
  • Weeks 8–10: Productivity Software: Introduction To Electronic Spreadsheet
    B9.2.4.1. Demonstrate How to Use Spreadsheet (Advanced Operations)
  • Week 11: Communication Networks: Computer Networks
    B9.3.1.1. Know the Concept of Computer Networking for Global Communications
  • Week 12: Communication Networks: Internet And Social Media
    B9.3.2.1. Demonstrate the Use of Social Networking and Electronic Mail

Term 3

  • Weeks 2–3: Communication Networks: Information Security
    B9.3.3.1. Recognise data threats and the means of protection
  • Weeks 4–6: Communication Networks: Web Technologies
    B9.3.4.1. Demonstrate the Use of a Web Browser (Blogging)
  • Weeks 7–8: Computational Thinking: Introduction To Programming
    B9.4.1.1. Show an Understanding of the Concept of Programming
  • Weeks 9–10: Computational Thinking: Algorithm
    B9.4.2.1. Analyse the Correct Step-by-step Procedure in Solving any Real-world Problem
  • Week 11: Computational Thinking: Robotics
    B9.4.3.1. Discuss Robot Intelligence Concepts
  • Week 12: Computational Thinking: Artificial Intelligence
    B9.4.4.1. Discuss Artificial intelligence Concepts
