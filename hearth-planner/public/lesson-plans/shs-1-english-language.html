What SHS 1 English Language covers

Topics by week, from the scheme of learning that GuidEd uses to write each plan.

Term 1

  • Weeks 2–6: 1.1 Listening Comprehension
    SHS1.1.1.1 Demonstrate the ability to listen to extended texts and extract specific information
  • Weeks 7–10: 1.2 Public Speaking and Conversation
    SHS1.1.2.1 Demonstrate effective communication skills in formal and informal contexts
  • Weeks 11–12: 2.2 Reading Comprehension
    SHS1.2.2.1 Analyze complex narrative, expository, and argumentative texts

Term 2

  • Weeks 2–3: 2.2 Reading Comprehension
    SHS1.2.2.1 Analyze complex narrative, expository, and argumentative texts
  • Weeks 4–7: 2.3 Summary
    SHS1.2.3.1 Demonstrate the ability to condense long passages accurately
  • Weeks 8–11: 3.1 Grammar
    SHS1.3.1.1 Apply advanced grammatical structures correctly in speech and writing
  • Week 12: 4.1 Narrative and Descriptive Writing
    SHS1.4.1.1 Demonstrate proficiency in creative and descriptive writing

Term 3

  • Weeks 2–4: 4.1 Narrative and Descriptive Writing
    SHS1.4.1.1 Demonstrate proficiency in creative and descriptive writing
  • Weeks 5–8: 4.2 Argumentative and Expository Writing
    SHS1.4.2.1 Compose well-structured formal essays, letters, and reports
  • Weeks 9–12: 5.1 Appreciation of Prose, Poetry, and Drama
    SHS1.5.1.1 Analyze literary texts prescribed for study
