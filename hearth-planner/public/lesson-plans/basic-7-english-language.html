What Basic 7 English Language covers

Topics by week, from the scheme of learning that GuidEd uses to write each plan.

Term 2

  • Week 2: Reading: Summarising
    B7.2.2.2. Demonstrate understanding of textual evidence that supports a writing piece
  • Weeks 3–9: Grammar Usage: Grammar
    B7.3.1.1. Apply the knowledge of word classes and their functions in Communication.; B7.3.1.2. Demonstrate command of structural and functional use of sentences; B7.3.1.3. Demonstrate command of structural and functional use of sentences.
  • Week 10: Grammar: Punctuation And Capitalisation
    B7.3.2.1. Demonstrate use and mastery of capitalisation and punctuation in communication
  • Week 11: Grammar: Vocabulary
    B7.3.3.1. Demonstrate appropriate use of vocabulary in communication

Term 3

  • Week 2: Grammar: Vocabulary
    B7.3.4.1. Demonstrate understanding of use of aesthetic language to enrich communication
  • Weeks 3–4: Writing: Production And
    B7.4.1.1. Develop, organise and express ideas coherently and cohesively in writing; B7.4.1.2. Create different paragraphs on a given topic
  • Weeks 5–9: Writing: Text Types And Purposes
    B7.4.2.1. Develop, organise and express ideas coherently and cohesively in writing for a variety of purposes (description, narration, exposition/information and persuasion), audiences and contexts; B7.4.2.2. Apply writing skills to specific life situations
  • Week 10: Writing: Building And Presenting Knowledge
    B7.4.3.1. Research to build and present knowledge
  • Week 11: Literature: Narrative, Drama And Poetry
    B7.5.1.1. Demonstrate understanding of how various elements of literary genres contribute to meaning
