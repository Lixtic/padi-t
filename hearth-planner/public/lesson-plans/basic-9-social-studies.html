What Basic 9 Social Studies covers

Topics by week, from the scheme of learning that GuidEd uses to write each plan.

Term 2

  • Week 2: Sense Of Purpose: Culture And National Identity
    B9.3.3.1. Evaluate the place of culture in national identity
  • Weeks 3–4: Law And Order: The 1992 Constitution
    B9.4.3.1. Assess the relevance of the 1992 Constitution
  • Week 5: Law And Order: Peace And Security In Our Nation
    B9.4.4.1. Assess the role of peace and security in national development
  • Weeks 6–8: Law And Order: Promoting Democracy And Political Stability
    B9.4.5.1. Show understanding of how to promote democracy and political stability; B9.4.5.2. Demonstrate understanding of the District Assembly concept in Ghana; B9.4.5.3. Evaluate the importance of political stability in Ghana’s development

Term 3

  • Week 2: Socio-Economic Development: Financial And Investment Issues
    B9.5.2.1. Show understanding of the provisions under National Pensions Act 766 and PNDC Law 247
  • Week 3: Socio-Economic Development: Science And Technology
    B9.5.4.1. Analyse the contribution of science and technology to national development
  • Weeks 4–7: Nationhood: The Republics
    B9.6.1.1. Demonstrate; B9.6.2.1. Demonstrate understanding that Ghana had two republics between 1979 and 2000
