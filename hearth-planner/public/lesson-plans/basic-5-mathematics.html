What Basic 5 Mathematics covers

Topics by week, from the scheme of learning that GuidEd uses to write each plan.

Term 1

  • Weeks 2–6: 1. Number: 1.1 Counting, Representation, Cardinality & Ordinality
    B5.1.1.1 Demonstrate an understanding of quantities and place value for numbers up to 10,000
  • Weeks 7–11: 1. Number: 1.2 Operations
    B5.1.2.1 Apply mental and written strategies to solve addition and subtraction problems
  • Week 12: 1. Number: 1.3 Fractions
    B5.1.3.1 Demonstrate understanding of fractions as numbers

Term 2

  • Weeks 2–5: 1. Number: 1.3 Fractions
    B5.1.3.1 Demonstrate understanding of fractions as numbers
  • Weeks 6–10: 2. Algebra: 2.1 Patterns and Relationships
    B5.2.1.1 Recognize, create and extend geometric and numeric patterns
  • Weeks 11–12: 3. Geometry and Measurement: 3.1 2D and 3D Shapes
    B5.3.1.1 Describe and classify 2D shapes and 3D objects

Term 3

  • Weeks 2–4: 3. Geometry and Measurement: 3.1 2D and 3D Shapes
    B5.3.1.1 Describe and classify 2D shapes and 3D objects
  • Weeks 5–8: 3. Geometry and Measurement: 3.2 Measurement
    B5.3.2.1 Estimate and measure length, mass, and capacity
  • Weeks 9–12: 4. Data: 4.1 Data Collection, Organization and Interpretation
    B5.4.1.1 Collect, organize, and represent data
