Basic 4 English Language lesson plans and scheme of work

Below is the Basic 4 English Language scheme of work, week by week, and you can make a ready-to-teach weekly lesson plan that follows the Ghana Education Service (GES) and NaCCA scheme of learning. What Basic 4 English Language covers

Topics by week, from the scheme of learning that GuidEd uses to write each plan.

Term 1

  • Weeks 2–6: 1. Oral Language: 1.1 Songs, Poems, Rhymes and Drama
    B4.1.1.1 Demonstrate understanding of songs, poems, rhymes and drama
  • Weeks 7–10: 1. Oral Language: 1.2 Listening and Comprehension
    B4.1.2.1 Demonstrate the ability to listen to extended texts and extract key information
  • Weeks 11–12: 2. Reading: 2.1 Phonics and Word Knowledge
    B4.2.1.1 Demonstrate understanding of phonics and word building

Term 2

  • Weeks 2–3: 2. Reading: 2.1 Phonics and Word Knowledge
    B4.2.1.1 Demonstrate understanding of phonics and word building
  • Weeks 4–7: 2. Reading: 2.2 Comprehension
    B4.2.2.1 Demonstrate the ability to read and understand texts
  • Weeks 8–11: 3. Grammar: 3.1 Grammar and Usage
    B4.3.1.1 Demonstrate understanding of parts of speech
  • Week 12: 3. Grammar: 3.2 Punctuation
    B4.3.2.1 Demonstrate understanding of punctuation marks

Term 3

  • Weeks 2–4: 3. Grammar: 3.2 Punctuation
    B4.3.2.1 Demonstrate understanding of punctuation marks
  • Weeks 5–8: 4. Writing: 4.1 Composition
    B4.4.1.1 Demonstrate the ability to write effectively
  • Weeks 9–12: 5. Literature: 5.1 Appreciating Literature
    B4.5.1.1 Demonstrate appreciation for literature
