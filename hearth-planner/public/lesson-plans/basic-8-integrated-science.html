What Basic 8 Integrated Science covers

Topics by week, from the scheme of learning that GuidEd uses to write each plan.

Term 2

  • Week 2: Systems: The Human Body System
    B8.3.1.1. Demonstrate knowledge of parts of mammalian tooth and the functions of the different types of teeth in relation to feeding in man
  • Week 3: Systems: The Solar System / Ecosystem
    B8.3.2.1. Demonstrate knowledge of the outer planets of the solar system | B8.3.3.1. Demonstrate an understanding of the interdependence of organisms in an ecosystem and their interaction
  • Week 4: Systems: Farming Systems
    B8.3.4.1. Demonstrate understanding of the different crop, animal and land combinations under various farming systems
  • Weeks 5–7: Forces And Energy: Energy
    B8.4.1.1. Demonstrate the skill to evaluate the conversion of energy from one form to another; B8.4.1.2. Show an understanding of the sources of renewable energy and how to manage these sources in a sustainable manner; B8.4.1.3. Demonstrate an understanding of the relationship between heat and temperature.
  • Weeks 8–9: Forces And Energy: Electricity And Electronics
    B8.4.2.1. Demonstrate knowledge of electricity transmission; B8.4.2.2. Demonstrate understanding of the functions of capacitors in relation to LEDs, Diodes and resistors in electronic circuits
  • Week 10: Forces And Energy: Conversion And Conservation Of Energy
    B8.4.3.1. Evaluate the impact of conversion of energy and energy conservation on the environment
  • Week 11: Forces And Energy: Force And Motion
    B8.4.4.1. Demonstrate the production of magnet, domestic and industrial application of Magnetic force and its relationship with Newton’s Second law of motion and in everyday life

Term 3

  • Weeks 2–3: Forces And Energy: Force And Motion
    B8.4.4.1. Demonstrate the production of magnet, domestic and industrial application of Magnetic force and its relationship with Newton’s Second law of motion and in everyday life; B8.4.4.2. Demonstrate understanding of complex machines and how they work
  • Week 4: Forces And Energy: Agricultural Tools
    B8.4.5.1. Demonstrate knowledge and skills in the use of basic and simple agricultural tools for basic on-farm activities
  • Week 5: Humans And The Environment: Waste Management
    B8.5.1.1. Demonstrate knowledge of waste management systems and apply it in an environment
  • Weeks 6–7: Humans And The Environment: Human Health
    B8.5.2.1. Demonstrate knowledge of common communicable diseases, such as Hepatitis, of humans, causes, symptoms, effects and their prevention; B8.5.2.2. Demonstrate knowledge of the nature of selected bacterial diseases of humans, their causes, symptoms, effects and prevention
  • Week 8: Humans And The Environment: Science And Industry
    B8.5.3.1. Demonstrate an understanding of connections among science, technology, innovation, society and the environment
  • Week 9: Humans And The Environment: Climate Change And Green Economy
    B8.5.4.1. Demonstrate an understanding of the effects of climate change in the world and greening of other tropical countries including Ghana.
  • Weeks 10–11: Humans And The Environment: Understanding The Environment
    B8.5.5.1. Demonstrate understanding of the differences among soils, plant roots, stems, leaves, flowers, and fruits of plants in the different environments; B8.5.6.1. Recognise the different types of rocks as origin of different types of soils
