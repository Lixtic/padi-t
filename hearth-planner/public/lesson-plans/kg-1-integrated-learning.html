KG 1 Integrated Learning lesson plans and scheme of work

Below is the KG 1 Integrated Learning scheme of work, week by week, and you can make a ready-to-teach weekly lesson plan that follows the Ghana Education Service (GES) and NaCCA scheme of learning. You get two lessons, teacher's notes and Oral Check with a marking guide, as a Word file you can edit.

What KG 1 Integrated Learning covers

Topics by week, from the scheme of learning that GuidEd uses to write each plan.

Term 1

  • Week 2: All About Me: 1.1 I am a wonderful and unique creation
    K1.1.1.1 Demonstrate the understanding that all learners are wonderful and have unique body features that make them different from other people and other God’s creation in the environment.
  • Week 3: All About Me: 1.2 The parts of the human body and their functions
    K1.1.2.1
  • Week 4: All About Me: 1.3 Caring for the parts of my body
    K1.1.3.1
  • Weeks 5–6: All About Me: 1.4 Keeping my body healthy: good food and vaccination
    K1.1.4.1 Demonstrate the understanding that eating good food and taking all my childhood vaccinations will keep me growing healthy and strong.
  • Week 7: All About Me: 1.5 My environment and my health
    K1.1.5.1 Demonstrate understanding of how keeping our home and school environment clean can keep us healthy and strong.
  • Week 8: All About Me: 1.6 Protecting ourselves from accidents
    K1.1.6.1 Demonstrate understanding of measures to take to keep safe (from accidents, hurts, and harms).
  • Week 9: My Family: 2.1 Types and members of my family
    K1.2.1.1 Demonstrate understanding of the importance, roles and responsibilities of family members.
  • Week 10: My Family: 2.2 Origin and history of my family
    K1.2.2.1 Demonstrate knowledge of the origin and history of our families.
  • Week 11: My Family: 2.3 Family celebrations and festivals
    K1.2.3.1 Demonstrate the knowledge of celebrations and festivals that the individual families celebrate.
  • Week 12: My Family: 2.4 My school family: rules and regulations
    K1.2.4.1 Demonstrate understanding of the rules and regulations that governs the members of the school

Term 2

  • Weeks 2–3: Values and Beliefs: 3.1 Our family values
    K1.3.1.1 Demonstrate understanding of the good manners that our families values and why they value them.
  • Week 4: Values and Beliefs: 3.2 Our cultural and family values
    K1.3.2.1
  • Weeks 5–6: Values and Beliefs: 3.3 Our religious values
    K1.3.3.1 Demonstrate understanding of the religious and moral values and virtues we need to exhibit as good Christians and Muslims
  • Week 7: Values and Beliefs: 3.4 Our beliefs
    K1.3.4.1 Demonstrate understanding of and relating well with people with different beliefs
  • Week 8: My Local Community: 4.1 Special places in our local community
    K1.4.1.1 Demonstrate understanding of the special places in our local communities .
  • Week 9: My Local Community: 4.2 Important people and occupations in our community
    K1.4.2.1 Demonstrate knowledge of the people in the community and their occupation.
  • Week 10: My Local Community: 4.3 Special leaders in our community and country
    K1.4.3.1 Demonstrate understanding and knowledge of the special leaders and their roles in our communities.
  • Weeks 11–12: My Nation Ghana: 5.1 History and celebration of Ghana's independence
    K1.5.1.1 Demonstrate knowledge of the history and independence of Ghana

Term 3

  • Week 2: All Around Us: 6.1 Living and non-living things
    K1.6.1.1 Demonstrate understanding of living and non-living things.
  • Week 3: All Around Us: 6.2 Living things: domestic and wild animals
    K1.6.2.1 Demonstrate understanding of domestic and wild animals. K1.6.2.1.1Sing and talk about different kinds of animals, what they eat, the sound they make and put them into groups.
  • Weeks 4–5: All Around Us: 6.3 Water
    K1.6.3.1 Demonstrate understanding of sources of water and its uses.
  • Week 6: All Around Us: 6.4 Air
    K1.6.4.1 Demonstrate understanding of the presence of air and tell its importance.
  • Week 7: All Around Us: 6.5 Plants 1
    K1.6.5.1 Demonstrate understanding of the parts of plants and their functions.
  • Week 8: All Around Us: 6.6 Plants 2
    K1.6.6.1 Demonstrate understanding of how plants grow
  • Week 9: All Around Us: 6.7 Gardening
    K1.6.7.1 Demonstrate understanding of the types of soil.
  • Week 10: All Around Us: 6.8 Light: day and night
    K1.6.8.1 Demonstrate understanding of the sources of light for day and night.
  • Week 11: All Around Us: 6.9 Changing weather conditions
    K1.6.9.1 Demonstrate understanding of changing weather conditions and seasons.
  • Week 12: My Global Community: 7.1 Connecting and communicating with the global community
    K1.7.1.1 Demonstrate understanding of different ways we connect and communicate with the global world.
