What Basic 9 Physical Education and Health covers

Topics by week, from the scheme of learning that GuidEd uses to write each plan.

Term 1

  • Weeks 2–5: Health Education: Nutrition And Physical Activity
    B9.1.1.1. Demonstrate understanding of nutritional problems associated with participation in sports physical activities
  • Weeks 6–7: Health Education: Disease Prevention And Management
    B9.1.2.1. Discuss ways to prevent diseases associated with sedentary behaviours and physical inactivity
  • Weeks 8–9: Health Education: First Aid, Injury Prevention And Management
    B9.1.3.1. Apply internationally accepted best practices for injury management in sports and physical activity settings
  • Weeks 10–12: Physical Activity Education: Traditional Rhythmic Gymnastics, Games And Dance
    B9.2.1.1. Demonstrate understanding of performing a variety of traditional games and adaptations for inclusivity and cultural identity; B9.2.1.2. Demonstrate understanding of performing a variety of traditional dances and adaptations for inclusivity and cultural identity

Term 2

  • Weeks 2–4: Physical Activity Education: Traditional Rhythmic Gymnastics, Games And Dance
    B9.2.1.3. Demonstrate understanding of performing a variety of traditional rhythmic gymnastics and adaptations for inclusivity and cultural identity
  • Weeks 5–12: Physical Activity Education: Physical Fitness
    B9.2.2.1. Demonstrate ability to maintain or improve the level of participation in a variety of muscular strength and endurance activities; B9.2.2.2. Demonstrate ability to maintain or improve the level of participation in a variety of cardiorespiratory strength and endurance activities; B9.2.2.3. Demonstrate the ability to maintain or improve the level of participation in a variety of flexibility and balance activities

Term 3

  • Weeks 2–12: Physical Activity Education: Organised Sports And Physical Activity Participation
    B9.2.3.1. Demonstrate the ability to apply movement concepts, principles and strategies in performing non–contact sports (e.g., ball and racket, athletics, individual and target sports); B9.2.3.2. Demonstrate the ability to apply movement principles and strategies in performing limited– contact sports (e.g., ball and stick and hand and ball sports); B9.2.3.3. Demonstrate the ability to apply movement principles and strategies in performing full– contact sports (e.g., invasion, martial art, and traditional wrestling)
