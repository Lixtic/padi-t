What Basic 7 Career Technology covers

Topics by week, from the scheme of learning that GuidEd uses to write each plan.

Term 2

  • Week 2: Materials For Production: Food Commodities (Animal And Plant Sources)
    B7.2.4.1. Demonstrate knowledge of basic food commodities
  • Weeks 3–4: Tools, Equipment And Processes: Measuring And Marking Out
    B7.3.1.1. Demonstrate understanding of measuring and marking out tools and equipment for production CONT’D
  • Weeks 5–6: Tools, Equipment And Processes: Cutting/Shaping
    B7.3.2.1. Demonstrate understanding of cutting/shaping tools and equipment for production CONT’D
  • Week 7: Tools, Equipment And Processes: Joining And Assembling
    B7.3.3.1. Demonstrate understanding of joining and assembling materials, tools and equipment used for production CONT’D
  • Week 8: Tools, Equipment And Processes: Kitchen Essentials
    B7.3.4.1. Demonstrate knowledge and understanding of the Kitchen Essentials
  • Week 9: Tools, Equipment And Processes: Finishes And Finishing
    B7.3.5.1. Demonstrate knowledge of finishes and finishing CONT’D
  • Weeks 10–11: Technology: Simple Structures And Mechanisms, Electric And Electronic Systems
    B7.4.1.1. Demonstrate understanding of structures in construction CONT’D

Term 3

  • Weeks 2–5: Designing And Making Of Artefacts/Products: Communicating Designs
    B7.5.1.1. Demonstrate knowledge and skills of drawing materials, instruments/equipment, lines and their applications, and freehand sketching CONT’D
  • Week 6: Designing And Making Of Artefacts/Products: Designing
    B7.5.2.1. Demonstrate understanding of Designing
  • Week 7: Designing And Making Of Artefacts/Products: Planning For Making Artefacts/Products
    B7.5.3.1. Demonstrate understanding of planning for making artefacts/products
  • Weeks 8–9: Designing And Making Of Artefacts/Products: Making Artefacts From Compliant, Resistant Materials And Food Ingredients
    B7.5.4.1. Demonstrate skills of making artefacts/ products CONT’D
  • Weeks 10–11: Entrepreneurial Skills: Establishing And Managing A Small Business Enterprise
    B7.6.2.1. Demonstrate understanding of Establishing and Managing a Small Business Enterprise CONT’D
