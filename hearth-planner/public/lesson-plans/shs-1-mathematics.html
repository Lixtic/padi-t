What SHS 1 Mathematics covers

Topics by week, from the scheme of learning that GuidEd uses to write each plan.

Term 1

  • Weeks 2–6: 1.1 Real Numbers
    SHS1.1.1.1 Demonstrate an understanding of real number systems, surds, and indices
  • Weeks 7–11: 1.2 Modular Arithmetic
    SHS1.1.2.1 Demonstrate understanding of modular arithmetic and its applications
  • Week 12: 2.1 Algebraic Expressions
    SHS1.2.1.1 Expand, factorize, and simplify algebraic expressions and fractions

Term 2

  • Weeks 2–5: 2.1 Algebraic Expressions
    SHS1.2.1.1 Expand, factorize, and simplify algebraic expressions and fractions
  • Weeks 6–10: 2.2 Equations and Inequalities
    SHS1.2.2.1 Solve linear, simultaneous, and quadratic equations and inequalities
  • Weeks 11–12: 3.1 Plane Geometry
    SHS1.3.1.1 Apply theorems of angles, polygons, and circles to solve problems

Term 3

  • Weeks 2–4: 3.1 Plane Geometry
    SHS1.3.1.1 Apply theorems of angles, polygons, and circles to solve problems
  • Weeks 5–8: 3.2 Mensuration
    SHS1.3.2.1 Calculate perimeters, areas, and volumes of regular and composite shapes
  • Weeks 9–12: 4.1 Statistics
    SHS1.4.1.1 Collect, organize, and represent data graphically
