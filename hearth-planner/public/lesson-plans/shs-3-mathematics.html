What SHS 3 Mathematics covers

Topics by week, from the scheme of learning that GuidEd uses to write each plan.

Term 1

  • Weeks 2–8: 2. Algebra: 2.3 Variation
    SHS3.2.3.1 Solve problems involving direct, inverse, joint, and partial variation
  • Weeks 9–12: 2. Algebra: 2.4 Linear Programming
    SHS3.2.4.1 Solve linear inequalities graphically and optimize objective functions

Term 2

  • Weeks 2–4: 2. Algebra: 2.4 Linear Programming
    SHS3.2.4.1 Solve linear inequalities graphically and optimize objective functions
  • Weeks 5–11: 3. Geometry and Measurement: 3.3 Vectors and Mechanics
    SHS3.3.3.1 Perform vector operations and solve basic mechanics problems
  • Week 12: 5. Calculus: 5.1 Differentiation
    SHS3.5.1.1 Understand the concept of limits and derivative of functions

Term 3

  • Weeks 2–6: 5. Calculus: 5.1 Differentiation
    SHS3.5.1.1 Understand the concept of limits and derivative of functions
  • Weeks 7–12: 5. Calculus: 5.2 Integration
    SHS3.5.2.1 Understand integration as the reverse of differentiation
