What Basic 7 Creative Arts and Design covers

Topics by week, from the scheme of learning that GuidEd uses to write each plan.

Term 1

  • Week 2: Design: Design in Nature and Manmade Environment / Drawing, Shading, Colouring and Modelling for Design
    B7/JHS 1.1.1 Demonstrate understanding of design concepts B7/JHS1 1.2.1 Demonstrate understanding of drawing, shading, colouring, modelling
  • Week 3: Design: Creativity, Innovation and the Design Process
    B7/JHS1 1.3.1 Demonstrate understanding of creativity & innovation in design process
  • Week 4: Creative Arts (Visual Arts): Media and Techniques
    B7/JHS 2.1.1 Visual Arts: Media and techniques in still-life drawing, pattern making, modelling
  • Week 5: Creative Arts (Performing Arts - Music): Media and Techniques
    B7/JHS1. 2.1.2 Music: Scale, note durational values, beat patterns
  • Week 6: Creative Arts (Dance and Drama): Media and Techniques
    B7/JHS1. 2.1.3 Dance and Drama: Apply media, voice, movement techniques
  • Week 7: Creative Arts (Visual Arts): Creative and Aesthetic Expression
    B7/JHS1. 2.2.1 Visual Arts: Use design process to produce, display, appraise artworks
  • Week 8: Creative Arts (Performing Arts - Music): Creative and Aesthetic Expression
    B7/JHS1. 2.3.2 Music: Correlate and generate ideas from indigenous musical forms/musicians
  • Week 9: Creative Arts (Dance and Drama): Creative and Aesthetic Expression
    B7/JHS1. 2.2.3 Dance and Drama: Produce dance and drama artworks
  • Week 10: Creative Arts (Visual Arts): Connections in Local and Global Cultures
    B7/JHS1. 2.3.1 Visual Arts: Correlate/generate ideas from community visual artworks
  • Week 11: Creative Arts (Performing Arts - Music): Connections in Local and Global Cultures
    B7/JHS1. 2.3.2 Music: Correlate and generate ideas from indigenous musical forms/musicians
  • Week 12: Creative Arts (Dance and Drama): Connections in Local and Global Cultures
    B7/JHS1. 2.3.3 Dance and Drama: Correlate ideas from dancers and actors

Term 2

  • Weeks 2–8: Creative Arts: 1: Media And Techniques
    B7.2.1.1. Visual Arts: Demonstrate understanding of relevant Visual Arts media and techniques and their application to still-life drawing, shading, pattern making and modelling; B7.2.1.2. Performing Arts - Music: Demonstrate understanding and apply scale, note durational values and simple time beat patterns in music.; B7.2.1.3. Dance and Drama: Demonstrate understanding and apply media, voice and movement techniques in dance and drama
  • Weeks 9–12: Creative Arts: 2.2. Creative And Aesthetic Expression
    B7.2.2.1. Visual Arts: Demonstrate the ability to use concept of the design process (idea development) to produce, display and appraise own creative artworks that reflect in the range of different times and cultures; B7.2.2.2. Performing Arts - Music: Demonstrate the ability to use concept of the design process (idea development) to create and display own creative musical art works that reflect the range of different times and cultures

Term 3

  • Weeks 2–6: Creative Arts: 2.2. Creative And Aesthetic Expression
    B7.2.2.2. Performing Arts - Music: Demonstrate the ability to use concept of the design process (idea development) to create and display own creative musical art works that reflect the range of different times and cultures; B7.2.2.3. Dance and Drama: Demonstrate the ability to use concepts of design process (idea development) to produce and display own creative and expressive art-forms that reflect in the range of different times and cultures in dance and drama
  • Weeks 7–12: Creative Arts: 2.3. Connections In Local And Global Cultures
    B7.2.3.1. Visual Arts: Demonstrate the ability to correlate and generate ideas from visual artworks in the community that reflect a range of different times, cultures and topical issues; B7.2.3.2. Performing Arts - Music: Demonstrate the ability to correlate and generate ideas from indigenous creative musical forms and art musicians in the immediate community that reflect a range of different times, cultures and topical issues; B7.2.3.3. Dance and Drama: Demonstrate the ability to correlate and generate ideas from creative artworks of dancers and actors in the community that reflect a range of different times, cultures and topical issues.
