What Basic 7 Creative Arts and Design covers

Topics by week, from the scheme of learning that GuidEd uses to write each plan.

Term 1

  • Week 2: Design: Design in Nature and Manmade Environment / Drawing, Shading, Colouring and Modelling for Design
    B7/JHS 1.1.1 Demonstrate understanding of design concepts B7/JHS1 1.2.1 Demonstrate understanding of drawing, shading, colouring, modelling
  • Week 3: Design: Creativity, Innovation and the Design Process
    B7/JHS1 1.3.1 Demonstrate understanding of creativity & innovation in design process
  • Week 4: Creative Arts (Visual Arts): Media and Techniques
    B7/JHS 2.1.1 Visual Arts: Media and techniques in still-life drawing, pattern making, modelling
  • Week 5: Creative Arts (Performing Arts - Music): Media and Techniques
    B7/JHS1. 2.1.2 Music: Scale, note durational values, beat patterns
  • Week 6: Creative Arts (Dance and Drama): Media and Techniques
    B7/JHS1. 2.1.3 Dance and Drama: Apply media, voice, movement techniques
  • Week 7: Creative Arts (Visual Arts): Creative and Aesthetic Expression
    B7/JHS1. 2.2.1 Visual Arts: Use design process to produce, display, appraise artworks
  • Week 8: Creative Arts (Performing Arts - Music): Creative and Aesthetic Expression
    B7/JHS1. 2.3.2 Music: Correlate and generate ideas from indigenous musical forms/musicians
  • Week 9: Creative Arts (Dance and Drama): Creative and Aesthetic Expression
    B7/JHS1. 2.2.3 Dance and Drama: Produce dance and drama artworks
  • Week 10: Creative Arts (Visual Arts): Connections in Local and Global Cultures
    B7/JHS1. 2.3.1 Visual Arts: Correlate/generate ideas from community visual artworks
  • Week 11: Creative Arts (Performing Arts - Music): Connections in Local and Global Cultures
    B7/JHS1. 2.3.2 Music: Correlate and generate ideas from indigenous musical forms/musicians
  • Week 12: Creative Arts (Dance and Drama): Connections in Local and Global Cultures
    B7/JHS1. 2.3.3 Dance and Drama: Correlate ideas from dancers and actors
