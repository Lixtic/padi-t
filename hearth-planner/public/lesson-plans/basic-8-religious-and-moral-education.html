What Basic 8 Religious and Moral Education covers

Topics by week, from the scheme of learning that GuidEd uses to write each plan.

Term 1

  • Weeks 2–3: God, His Creation And Attributes: The Creation Stories Of The Three Major Religions In Ghana
    B8.1.1.1. Outline and explain the moral lessons in the creation stories of the three main religions in Ghana.
  • Weeks 4–9: Religious Practices: Rites Of Passage
    B8.2.1.1. Explain rites of passage and identify the moral lessons in them

Term 2

  • Weeks 2–4: The Family And The Community: Authority And Obedience
    B8.3.1.1. Identify and explain the importance of obeying authority
  • Weeks 5–7: Religious Leaders And Personalities: Prophets And Caliphs
    B8.4.1.1. Identify and explain the moral lessons that can be learned from the exemplary lives of the prophets and caliphs
  • Week 8: Ethics And Moral Life: Moral Teachings In The Three Major Religions In Ghana
    B8.5.1.1. Exemplify the moral teachings from the Bible, Qur’an and Oral Traditions

Term 3

  • Week 2: Ethics And Moral Life: Moral Teachings In The Three Major Religions In Ghana
    B8.5.1.1. Exemplify the moral teachings from the Bible, Qur’an and Oral Traditions
  • Weeks 3–6: Religion And Economic Life: Money
    B8.6.1.1. Plan the wise use of money
  • Weeks 7–8: Religion And Economic Life: Bribery And Corruption
    B8.6.2.1. Explain the need to avoid bribery and corruption and the ways to do so.
