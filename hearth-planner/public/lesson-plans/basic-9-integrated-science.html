What Basic 9 Integrated Science covers

Topics by week, from the scheme of learning that GuidEd uses to write each plan.

Term 2

  • Week 2: Systems: The Human Body System
    B9.3.1.1. Demonstrate understanding of the blood circulatory system, health problems associated with the system and its relationship with the respiratory system in humans
  • Week 3: Systems: The Solar System
    B9.3.2.1. Demonstrate knowledge of other non- planetary bodies such as comets, asteroids, and their relationship with the solar system
  • Week 4: Systems: Ecosystem
    B9.3.3.1. Recognise the interdependence of organisms in an ecosystem and appreciate their interaction to maintain balance in the system
  • Week 5: Systems: Farming Systems
    B9.3.4.1. Demonstrate knowledge and skills in the preparation of different types of manure from animal and plant waste
  • Weeks 6–8: Forces And Energy: Energy
    B9.4.1.1. Show understanding of the concept of conservation of energy and ways of conserving energy; B9.4.1.2. Demonstrate understanding in and the capability to do calculations involving energy.; B9.4.1.3. Evaluate the application of light energy in life.
  • Weeks 9–10: Forces And Energy: Electricity And Electronics
    B9.4.2.1. Construct electrical circuits and illustrate how electrical energy is transformed into other forms of energy and perform electrical calculations; B9.4.2.2. Demonstrate an understanding of Forward and Reverse Bias and explain the behaviour of LEDs, Diodes, Resistors and Capacitors in electronic circuits
  • Week 11: Forces And Energy: Conversion And Conservation Of Energy
    B9.4.3.1. Show an understanding of conversion and conservation of energy and their application to life

Term 3

  • Weeks 2–3: Forces And Energy: Force And Motion
    B9.4.4.1. Demonstrate understanding of the concept of pressure and explain how pressure acts in everyday life; B9.4.4.2. Demonstrate understanding of Newton’s Laws of motion and ability to apply the laws to solve problems in everyday life
  • Week 4: Forces And Energy: Agricultural Tools
    B9.4.5.1. Demonstrate knowledge and skills in making simple agricultural tools for on-farm activities
  • Week 5: Humans And The Environment: Waste Management
    B9.5.1.1. Demonstrate an understanding of the scientific ways of waste management | B9.5.1.2. Demonstrate an understanding of the impact of waste on an environment, innovative waste management technologies for sustainable development and waste management practices in Ghana
  • Weeks 6–7: Humans And The Environment: Human Health
    B9.5.2.1. Demonstrate knowledge of common non-communicable diseases of humans, their causes, symptoms, effects and prevention; B9.5.2.2. Demonstrate understanding of the relationship of health and disease, the concept of common diseases in the environment and how to control them
  • Weeks 8–9: Humans And The Environment: Science And Industry
    B9.5.3.1. Analyse the scientific concepts, principles and processes applied in industries in and outside their community; B9.5.3.2. Demonstrate an understanding of the concept of industry, the science underpinning the processes of production in industries the technologies in indigenous industries and western industries
  • Week 10: Humans And The Environment: Climate Change And Green Economy
    B9.5.4.1. Demonstrate an understanding of the natural and human factors that influence climate change and a green economy | B9.5.4.2. Evaluate the effectiveness of initiatives that address the issue of climate change and green economy in Ghana and the world at large
  • Week 11: Humans And The Environment: Understanding The Environment
    B9.5.5.1. Demonstrate knowledge and skills in the use of plant roots, stems, leaves, flowers, and fruits for agricultural and non-agricultural purposes
