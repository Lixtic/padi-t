What Basic 8 Physical Education and Health covers

Topics by week, from the scheme of learning that GuidEd uses to write each plan.

Term 1

  • Weeks 2–3: Health Education: Nutrition And Physical Activity
    B8.1.1.1. Demonstrate understanding of the factors that influence the choice of food and feeding habits in relation to participation in sports and physical activity
  • Weeks 4–5: Health Education: Disease Prevention And Management
    B8.1.2.1. Develop awareness of behaviours that promote regular participation in sports and physical activity
  • Weeks 6–9: Health Education: First Aid, Injury Prevention And Management
    B8.1.3.1. Apply the principles of first aid in a variety of sports and physical activity settings to support environmental and personal safety
  • Weeks 10–12: Physical Activity Education: Traditional Rhythmic Gymnastics, Games And Dance
    B8.2.1.1. Perform a variety of traditional games and adaptations for inclusivity and cultural awareness, sensitivity, and appreciation of other cultures (e.g., socio-relational games). Refer to Mathematics CS; B8.2.1.2. Perform a variety of traditional dances and adaptations for inclusivity and cultural awareness (e.g., individual and group dance movements) for personal development and communication

Term 2

  • Weeks 2–3: Physical Activity Education: Traditional Rhythmic Gymnastics, Games And Dance
    B8.2.1.3. Demonstrate understanding and apply skills and movement techniques in global and traditional rhythmic gymnastics movements and adaptations for coordination and appreciation of global cultures
  • Weeks 4–9: Physical Activity Education: Physical Fitness
    B8.2.2.1. Demonstrate the ability to increase the level of participation in a variety of muscular strength and endurance activities; B8.2.2.2. Demonstrate the ability to increase the level of participation in a variety of cardiorespiratory activities; B8.2.2.3. Demonstrate the ability to increase the level of participation in a variety of flexibility and balance activities
  • Weeks 10–12: Physical Activity Education: Organised Sports And Physical Activity Participation
    B8.2.3.1. Demonstrate the ability to apply movement concepts, principles, and strategies in performing non–contact sports (e.g., ball and racket, athletics, and, individual and target sports)

Term 3

  • Weeks 2–12: Physical Activity Education: Organised Sports And Physical Activity Participation
    B8.2.3.2. Demonstrate the ability to apply movement concepts, principles and strategies in performing limited–contact sports (e.g., stick and ball sports and hand and ball sports); B8.2.3.3. Demonstrate the ability to apply movement principles and strategies in performing full– contact sports (e.g., invasion sports, martial art, and traditional wrestling).
