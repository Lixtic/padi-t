What Basic 7 Religious and Moral Education covers

Topics by week, from the scheme of learning that GuidEd uses to write each plan.

Term 1

  • Weeks 2–4: God, His Creation And Attributes: God, His Nature And Attributes
    B7.1.1.1. Explain the nature of God seen through His attributes
  • Weeks 5–8: Religious Practices: Worship
    B7.2.1.1. Explain how worship is performed in the three major religions in Ghana and apply the moral lessons in the worship in their life.
  • Weeks 9–10: Religious Practices: Religious Songs And Recitations
    B7.2.2.1. Analyse and apply the moral values in religious songs and recitations

Term 2

  • Week 2: Religious Practices: Religious Songs And Recitations
    B7.2.2.1. Analyse and apply the moral values in religious songs and recitations
  • Weeks 3–5: The Family And The Community: Family Systems
    B7.3.1.1. Identify and Explain the Importance of the Family Systems
  • Weeks 6–8: Religious Leaders And Personalities: Religious Leaders
    B7.4.1.1. Identify the key features and moral messages of the call and ministry of Religious Leaders in the three major religions.
  • Weeks 9–10: Ethics And Moral Life: Manners And Decency
    B7.5.1.1. Develop good manners and learn how to apply them in their daily lives.

Term 3

  • Weeks 2–3: Ethics And Moral Life: Manners And Decency
    B7.5.1.1. Develop good manners and learn how to apply them in their daily lives.
  • Weeks 4–6: Ethics And Moral Life: Substance Abuse
    B7.5.2.1. Demonstrate the need to stay away from immoral thoughts and actions, with a key focus on substance abuse
  • Weeks 7–10: Religion And Economic Life: Work, Entrepreneurship And Social Security
    B7.6.1.1. Cultivate the need for hard work and develop the spirit of entrepreneurship
