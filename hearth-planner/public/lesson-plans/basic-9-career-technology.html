What Basic 9 Career Technology covers

Topics by week, from the scheme of learning that GuidEd uses to write each plan.

Term 2

  • Week 2: Tools, Equipment And Processes: Measuring And Marking Out
    B9.3.1.1. Demonstrate understanding of measuring and marking out tools and equipment for making artefacts/ products and care and maintain
  • Weeks 3–4: Tools, Equipment And Processes: Cutting/Shaping
    B9.3.2.1. Demonstrate the understanding of cutting/shaping tools and equipment used for making artefacts/ products and care and maintain
  • Week 5: Tools, Equipment And Processes: Joining And Assembling
    B9.3.3.1. Demonstrate understanding of materials. tools and equipment used for joining and assembling artefacts/products and care and maintain
  • Week 6: Tools, Equipment And Processes: Kitchen Essentials
    B9.3.4.1. Demonstrate skills of selecting and purchasing kitchen essentials and understanding and skills in the choice of basic kitchen essentials
  • Week 7: Tools, Equipment And Processes: Kitchen Essentials / Finishes And Finishing
    B9.3.5.1. Demonstrate understanding of application of finishes CONT’D
  • Weeks 8–9: Technology: Simple Structures And Mechanisms, Electric And Electronic Systems
    B9.4.1.1. Demonstrate understanding of applications of mechanisms in project constructions CONT’D
  • Weeks 10–11: Designing And Making Of Artefacts/Products: Communicating Designs
    B9.5.1.1. Demonstrate understanding of developing surfaces of objects for production/ manufacturing CONT’D; B9.5.1.2. Demonstrate understanding of orthographic projections

Term 3

  • Weeks 2–4: Designing And Making Of Artefacts/Products: Designing
    B9.5.2.1. Demonstrate understanding of orthographic projections CONT’D
  • Weeks 5–6: Designing And Making Of Artefacts/Products: Planning For Making Artefacts/Products
    B9.5.3.1. Demonstrate understanding of planning for making artefacts/ products/ meals
  • Weeks 7–8: Designing And Making Of Artefacts/Products: Making Artefacts From Compliant, Resistant Materials And Food Ingredients
    B9.5.4.1. Demonstrate understanding of gathering materials, tools and equipment for making/preparing meals and articles and evaluate CONT’D
  • Weeks 9–10: Entrepreneurial Skills: Career Pathways And Career Opportunities
    B9.6.1.1. Demonstrate understanding about the changing nature of the workplace, the value of work to society, and the connection of work to the achievement of personal goals CONT’D
  • Week 11: Entrepreneurial Skills: Establishing And Managing A Small Business Enterprise
    B9.6.2.1. Demonstrate understanding of establishing and managing a small business enterprise CONT’D
