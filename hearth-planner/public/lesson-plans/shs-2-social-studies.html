What SHS 2 Social Studies covers

Topics by week, from the scheme of learning that GuidEd uses to write each plan.

Term 1

  • Weeks 1–3: Identity Significance and Purpose: Identity and National Cohesion
    Demonstrate knowledge and understanding of Ghanaian values and traditions and how they promote or challenge national cohesion.
  • Weeks 4–5: Environment: Environmental Literacy and Sustainability
    Demonstrate knowledge and understanding of the interdependent relationship between humans and the physical environment and how they promote sustainable development.
  • Weeks 6–7: Law and Order in Society: Law Enforcement Mechanisms in Ghana
    Demonstrate knowledge and understanding of law enforcement in Ghana.
  • Weeks 8–10: Nationalism and Nationhood: European Encounter, Colonialism and Neo-Colonialism
    Demonstrate knowledge and understanding of European encounters and colonialism in Africa.
  • Weeks 11–12: Nationalism and Nationhood: Nationalism, Citizenship and Nation Building
    Demonstrate understanding of the origins and impact of Pan-Africanist ideals, highlighting how these ideals can be used to solve contemporary problems faced by African countries.

Term 2

  • Weeks 1–2: Ethics and Human Development: Leisure and Tourism
    Appreciate the relevance of leisure and recreation to personal and national development.
  • Weeks 3–4: Ethics and Human Development: Revolutions that Changed the World
    Demonstrate knowledge and understanding of the Scientific Revolution of the sixteenth century and its impact on the world.
  • Weeks 5–6: Ethics and Human Development: The Youth and National Development
    Demonstrate understanding of the expectations of the Ghanaian youth, the challenges they face and ways of addressing these challenges.
  • Week 7: Production, Exchange and Creativity: Economic Activities in Ghana
    Demonstrate knowledge and understanding of the concept of local production and consumption and their benefits to national development.
  • Weeks 8–9: Production, Exchange and Creativity: Entrepreneurship, Workplace Culture and Productivity
    Demonstrate knowledge and understanding of the relevance of an entrepreneur to national development.
  • Week 10: Production, Exchange and Creativity: Consumer Rights, Protection and Responsibilities
    Demonstrate knowledge and understanding of the various forms of consumer responsibilities towards the nation.
  • Weeks 11–12: Production, Exchange and Creativity: Financial Literacy
    Demonstrate knowledge and understanding of effective saving habits to achieve financial goals.

Term 3

  • Week 2: 3. Social Change and Development: 3.4 Population and Settlement
    SHS2.3.4.1 Understand population dynamics and settlement patterns in Ghana
  • Weeks 3–7: 4. Economic Development: 4.1 Agriculture and Industrialization
    SHS2.4.1.1 Understand the role of agriculture and industry in economic transformation
  • Weeks 8–12: 4. Economic Development: 4.2 Financial Literacy and Entrepreneurship
    SHS2.4.2.1 Demonstrate understanding of savings, investment, and entrepreneurship
