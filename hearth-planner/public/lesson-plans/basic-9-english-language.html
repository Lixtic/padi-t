What Basic 9 English Language covers

Topics by week, from the scheme of learning that GuidEd uses to write each plan.

Term 2

  • Weeks 2–3: Reading: Comprehension
    B9.2.1.2. Read, comprehend, and analyse varieties of texts
  • Week 4: Reading: Summarising
    B9.2.2.1. Cite the textual evidence that supports an analysis of what the text says, determining the central idea of a text and provide an objective summary
  • Weeks 5–9: Grammar Usage: Grammar
    B9.3.1.1. Apply the knowledge of phrases and clauses and their functions in Communication.; B9.3.1.2. Demonstrate understanding of the use of conditional tenses in communication; B9.3.1.3. Demonstrate command of structural and functional use of sentences
  • Week 10: Grammar: Punctuation And Capitalisation
    B9.3.2.1. Demonstrate mastery of capitalisation and punctuation in communication
  • Week 11: Grammar: Vocabulary
    B9.3.3.1. Demonstrate appropriate use of vocabulary and spelling conventions in communication

Term 3

  • Weeks 2–4: Writing: Production And Distribution Of Writing
    B9.4.1.1. Develop, organise and express ideas coherently and cohesively in writing; B9.4.1.2. Create different paragraphs a given topic (Please check for an omission here)
  • Weeks 5–8: Writing: Text Types And Purposes
    B9.4.2.1. Use a process approach to compose descriptive, narrative/ imaginative, informational and persuasive, argumentative texts; B9.4.2.2. Apply writing skills to specific life situations
  • Weeks 9–11: Writing: Building And Presenting Knowledge
    B9.4.3.1. Research to build and present knowledge; B9.5.1.1. Demonstrate understanding of how various elements of literary genres contribute to meaning
