What Basic 8 English Language covers

Topics by week, from the scheme of learning that GuidEd uses to write each plan.

Term 2

  • Weeks 2–4: Reading: Comprehension
    B8.2.1.2. Read, comprehend, interpret texts; B8.2.2.1. Cite the textual evidence that supports an analysis of a text to determine the central idea and provide an objective summary
  • Weeks 5–8: Grammar And Grammar Usage: Grammar
    B8.3.1.1. Apply the knowledge of word classes and their functions in Communication; B8.3.1.5. ; B8.3.1.6. Show understanding and use of question tags in communication
  • Week 9: Grammar: Punctuation And Capitalisation
    B8.3.2.1. Demonstrate mastery of capitalisation and punctuation in communication
  • Week 10: Grammar: Vocabulary
    B8.3.3.1. Demonstrate appropriate use of vocabulary in communication
  • Week 11: Writing: Production And Distribution Of Writing
    B8.4.1.1. Develop, organise and express ideas coherently and cohesively in writing

Term 3

  • Week 2: Writing: Production And Distribution Of Writing
    B8.4.1.2. Create different paragraphs within a composition on a given topic
  • Weeks 3–7: Writing: Text Types And Purposes
    B8.4.2.1. Use a process approach to compose descriptive, narrative/ imaginative, informational, persuasive and argumentative texts; B8.4.2.2. Apply writing skills to specific life situations
  • Week 8: Writing: Building And Present Knowledge
    B8.4.3.1. Research to build and present knowledge
  • Weeks 9–11: Literature: Narrative, Drama And Poetry
    B8.5.1.1. Demonstrate understanding of how various elements of literary genres contribute to meaning
