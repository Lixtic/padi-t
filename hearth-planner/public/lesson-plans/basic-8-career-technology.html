What Basic 8 Career Technology covers

Topics by week, from the scheme of learning that GuidEd uses to write each plan.

Term 2

  • Weeks 2–3: Tools, Equipment And Processes: Cutting/Shaping
    B8.3.2.1. Demonstrate understanding of cutting and shaping tools and equipment for making artefacts/products CONT’D
  • Weeks 4–6: Tools, Equipment And Processes: Joining And Assembling
    B8.3.3.1. Demonstrate understanding of joining and assembling materials, tools and equipment used for making artefacts/ products CONT’D
  • Week 7: Tools, Equipment And Processes: Kitchen Essentials
    B8.3.4.1. Demonstrate understanding of maintaining kitchen essentials
  • Week 8: Tools, Equipment And Processes: Finishes And Finishing
    B8.3.5.1. Demonstrate understanding of application of finishes CONT’D
  • Week 9: Technology: Simple Structures And Mechanisms, Electric And Electronic Systems
    B8.4.1.1. Demonstrate understanding of application of principles of forces acting on structures CONT’D
  • Week 10: Designing And Making Of Artefacts/Products: Communicating Design
    B8.5.1.1. Demonstrate understanding of drawing plane figures and solid objects using drawing instruments
  • Week 11: Designing And Making Of Artefacts/Products: Designing
    B8.5.2.1. Demonstrate knowledge and skills of Designing CONT’D

Term 3

  • Weeks 2–5: Designing And Making Of Artefacts/Products: Designing
    B8.5.2.1. Demonstrate knowledge and skills of Designing CONT’D
  • Weeks 6–7: Designing And Making Of Artefacts/Products: Planning For Making Artefacts/Products
    B8.5.3.1. Demonstrate understanding of planning for making artefacts/products and table setting
  • Weeks 8–9: Designing And Making Of Artefacts/Products: Making Artefacts From Compliant, Resistant Materials And Food Ingredients
    B8.5.4.1. Demonstrate understanding of designing artefacts/ products, and models and table setting CONT’D
  • Week 10: Entrepreneurial Skills: Career Pathways And Career Opportunities
    B8.6.1.1. Demonstrate knowledge of career opportunities in Career Technology
  • Week 11: Entrepreneurial Skills: Establishing And Managing A Small Business Enterprise
    B8.6.2.1. Demonstrate understanding of establishing and managing micro and small business enterprises
