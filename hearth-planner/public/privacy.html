Privacy

The short version: we collect only what GuidEd needs to work, we don't show ads, and we don't sell your data.

Last updated 7 November 2026

Using GuidEd without an account

  • Your plans, chats and settings (including the teacher and school name you type in) are saved on your own device, in your browser. They don't reach our servers unless you sign in.
  • To write a plan, what you enter (subject, class, week and any instructions or follow-up requests) is sent to our server and on to an AI service.
  • To limit daily previews, we keep a scrambled one-way code made from your connection's IP address. We don't store the address itself in that record.

If you create an account

  • We store your email address, your name if you give one, and your sign-in details. Passwords are handled by our sign-in provider, Neon Auth, and are not readable by us.
  • Your saved plans and chats are copied to our database so they appear on your other devices.
  • We record how many plans you make, for which class, subject and week, so we can apply the free and Pro limits.
  • We keep the date your account was first used, and add you to simple daily totals (such as plans written and payments made) that help us see how GuidEd is used. The totals don't name anyone.
  • If your Pro is about to end, we may email you once to remind you.

Feedback you send us

If you use "Report a bug or suggest an idea", we keep your message, the email you give (or your account email if you are signed in), your plan type, and technical details such as the app version, browser, screen size and, for sign-in problems, the sign-in step that failed. We never attach your plans or chats. We read these messages to fix problems and improve GuidEd, and can delete one on request.

Payments

Pro payments are handled by Paystack. You enter your card or mobile money details on Paystack, never on GuidEd. We keep the payment reference, your email, the amount, the status and the dates your Pro access runs, so we can unlock Pro and answer payment questions. Paystack has its own privacy policy.

The AI service

Plans are written by a third-party AI provider, one of Anthropic, OpenAI or Google depending on how GuidEd is set up. They receive your request and the plan text needed to answer it, and nothing else about you. Please don't type learners' names or other personal details into your requests. You don't need them for a lesson plan.

To save time and cost, a first plan for a common request (the same subject, class and week) may be stored and reused for other teachers. These stored plans are not linked to any account.

Cookies and tracking

  • We use one sign-in cookie, only if you sign in. It comes from GuidEd itself.
  • No advertising, no analytics trackers and no third-party cookies.
  • The app keeps a copy of its own files on your device so it loads quickly and works offline. "Clear app cache" in the menu removes it.

Who can see your data

Only us and the services that run GuidEd: our host (Vercel), our database and sign-in provider (Neon), Paystack for payments, and the AI provider above. We don't sell or rent your data. We may share information if the law requires it.

Your choices

  • Delete any plan or chat in the app. If you're signed in, the copy on our server goes too.
  • Sign out at any time. Plans already on a device stay there until you clear them.
  • Ask us to see, correct or delete your account and the data we hold. Use the contact below and we'll act on it within a reasonable time.

Keeping data safe

Connections are encrypted, and sign-in and payment checks happen on the server. No online service is perfectly secure, so keep your own copy of anything important by exporting it to Word.

Who GuidEd is for

GuidEd is for teachers and other adults. It isn't meant for children to use, and we don't knowingly collect children's information.

Changes

If we change this page in a way that matters, we'll update the date above. We aim to follow Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843).
