Privacy

The short version: we collect only what GuidEd needs to work, we show one ad on the start screen of the free version only, and we don't sell your data.

Last updated 18 November 2026

Using GuidEd without an account

  • Your plans, chats and settings (including the teacher and school name you type in) are saved on your own device, in your browser. They don't reach our servers unless you sign in.
  • To write a plan, what you enter (subject, class, week and any instructions or follow-up requests) is sent to our server and on to an AI service.
  • To limit daily previews, we keep a scrambled one-way code made from your connection's IP address. We don't store the address itself in that record.

If you create an account

  • We store your email address, your name if you give one, and your sign-in details. Passwords are handled by our sign-in provider, Neon Auth, and are not readable by us.
  • If you choose "Continue with Google", Google tells our sign-in provider your email address and name, and we use them to create or open your account. We never see your Google password, and we do not read your Gmail, contacts or anything else in your Google account.
  • Your saved plans and chats are copied to our database so they appear on your other devices.
  • We record how many plans you make, for which class, subject and week, so we can apply the free and Pro limits.
  • We keep the date your account was first used, and add you to simple daily totals (such as plans written and payments made) that help us see how GuidEd is used. The totals don't name anyone.
  • If your Pro is about to end, we may email you once to remind you.
  • To keep Sunday emails to once a week, we keep a small record of when one was sent. If you switch emails off, we keep a record that you did, so they stay off. Both are removed when you delete your account.

Feedback you send us

If you use "Report a bug or suggest an idea", we keep your message, the email you give (or your account email if you are signed in), your plan type, and technical details such as the app version, browser, screen size and, for sign-in problems, the sign-in step that failed. We never attach your plans or chats. We read these messages to fix problems and improve GuidEd, and can delete one on request.

Payments

Pro payments are handled by Paystack. Card details are entered on Paystack, never on GuidEd. If you choose to type your mobile money number in GuidEd, we save that number with your account so we can send the payment prompt straight to your phone next time, and we pass it to Paystack to charge you. You can clear it any time by emptying the field in the upgrade screen, and deleting your account removes it. We also keep the payment reference, your email, the amount, the status and the dates your Pro access runs, so we can unlock Pro and answer payment questions. Paystack has its own privacy policy.

The AI service

Plans are written by a third-party AI provider, one of Anthropic, OpenAI or Google depending on how GuidEd is set up. They receive your request and the plan text needed to answer it, and nothing else about you. Please don't type learners' names or other personal details into your requests. You don't need them for a lesson plan.

To save time and cost, a first plan for a common request (the same subject, class and week) may be stored and reused for other teachers. These stored plans are not linked to any account.

Cookies and tracking

  • We use one sign-in cookie, only if you sign in. It comes from GuidEd itself.
  • Teachers on the free version, and visitors who are not signed in, see one Google AdSense banner on the start screen. Pro teachers see none (see the Ads page). Free teachers who have used their week's plans can also choose to watch a short video ad for one extra plan; for that we keep a simple count of videos watched per day on your account, and Google's ad code runs only if you tap the button. We ask Google for non-personalised ads, but Google may still set cookies or use device identifiers to show and measure ads. You can use your browser or an ad blocker to refuse them, or choose Pro to remove ads. Ads never appear inside your lesson plans, Word files or shared files, and we never give advertisers your plans or your account details.
  • No analytics trackers.
  • The app keeps a copy of its own files on your device so it loads quickly and works offline, and it updates itself when you open it. "Clear app cache" in the menu removes it.
  • On your device we also remember a few settings, such as your theme, whether you use GuidEd as a parent or a teacher, and that you were signed in, so the app opens on the right screen. Signing out or clearing site data removes them.

Who can see your data

Only us and the services that run GuidEd: our host (Vercel), our database and sign-in provider (Neon), Paystack for payments, and the AI provider above. We don't sell or rent your data. We may share information if the law requires it.

Your choices

  • Delete any plan or chat in the app. If you're signed in, the copy on our server goes too. Parents can delete a single saved activity the same way. To keep the service fast, we keep up to 150 saved activities per parent account.
  • Sign out at any time. Plans already on a device stay there until you clear them.
  • Delete your account yourself: open your account in the app and choose "Delete my account". This removes your sign-in, saved plans, teacher or parent profile, children and their saved activities, usage counters and feedback you sent from our servers. We keep payment records for accounting, with your email address and account id removed, and a record that a blocked account was blocked so it cannot return by deleting itself. You can also ask us to see or correct your data. Use the contact below and we'll act on it within a reasonable time.

Keeping data safe

Connections are encrypted, and sign-in and payment checks happen on the server. No online service is perfectly secure, so keep your own copy of anything important by exporting it to Word.

Who GuidEd is for

GuidEd is for teachers and other adults. It isn't meant for children to use, and children don't create accounts or sign in.

Parents can add their children to a parent account, from Kindergarten to Basic 9. If you do, we keep each child's first name, class and chosen subjects, plus the home lessons and exercises you create or save for them, and any scores or notes you enter. We use this only to show you your own saved activities. You can change or remove a child at any time, which also deletes that child's saved activities, and deleting your account removes all of it. If you have children on your account, we email you one short learning idea each Sunday evening; the email also lists the activities each child finished that week, and you can switch it off in My Children or with the Unsubscribe link in the email. Please don't enter anything about a child that you wouldn't want stored.

Weekly teaching summary

If you have made lesson plans in the last week, we email you one short summary each Sunday evening: how many plans you made, which classes and subjects they were for, and the next scheme-of-learning week for the class you plan most. It uses only your saved plan details, never the lesson text. You can switch it off in your account settings or with the Unsubscribe link in the email.

Changes

If we change this page in a way that matters, we'll update the date above. We aim to follow Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843).
