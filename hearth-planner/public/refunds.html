Refund policy

GuidEd Pro is a one-off payment for a set period. Nothing renews on its own, so there is nothing to cancel. If Pro is not right for you, you can ask for your money back.

Within 7 days: full refund

If you ask within 7 days of paying, we refund the whole payment. You do not need to give a reason.

After 7 days

We still refund in full if you were charged twice, if you paid and Pro was not switched on, or if a fault on our side stopped you from using Pro for a good part of the time you paid for. Other requests after 7 days are looked at one by one.

How to ask

  1. Open GuidEd, tap your plan badge, then Need a refund or help with a payment? and send the message. Or write to us using the contact below.
  2. Tell us the email you signed in with, and the date and amount of the payment if you have them.

How you get the money

Refunds go back through Paystack to the card or mobile money account you paid with. We start the refund as soon as we have checked it, usually within 2 working days. Your bank or mobile money provider can then take up to 7 working days to show it.

What happens to Pro

When a payment is refunded, the Pro days it bought are taken off. Your account and your saved plans stay, and you go back to the free plan unless you have Pro time from another payment.

Your rights

This policy does not limit any rights you have under Ghanaian consumer law.
