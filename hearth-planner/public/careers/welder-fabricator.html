🔥 Welder and Metal Fabricator: career pathway in Ghana

Join and shape metal to make structures, gates, machinery parts and repairs.

Your pathway, step by step

  1. Build foundations. Learn measurement and reading simple drawings, and take workshop safety seriously.
  2. Choose a learning route. Explore an accredited TVET programme or a supervised apprenticeship in welding and fabrication.
  3. Practise with real work. Practise different joints and materials under supervision with proper protective equipment.
  4. Build evidence. Record assessed work and keep photos of finished projects.
  5. Enter and keep progressing. Work in construction, manufacturing or a fabrication shop, and specialise or start your own workshop.
