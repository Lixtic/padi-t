🎨 Animator and Digital Illustrator: career pathway in Ghana

Create moving images, characters and illustrations for films, games, adverts and learning.

Your pathway, step by step

  1. Build foundations. Practise drawing and storytelling, and learn how a short story is built from scenes.
  2. Choose a learning route. Explore graphics, animation or creative technology programmes, and try beginner animation and drawing software.
  3. Practise with real work. Make short animations or illustrated stories and share them for feedback.
  4. Build evidence. Collect your best clips in a portfolio, with notes on tools and time taken.
  5. Enter and keep progressing. Look for design studios, media houses and freelance work, and keep learning as tools change.

School subjects that help

  • Creative Arts
  • Computing / ICT
  • English Language

Skills to build

  • Drawing
  • Storytelling
  • Animation software
  • Meeting deadlines

A first step you can take now

Draw a character in four poses and make them into a one-second flip animation.
