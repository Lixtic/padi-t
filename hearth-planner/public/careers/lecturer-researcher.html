🔬 University Lecturer and Researcher: career pathway in Ghana

Teach at higher education level and carry out research.

Your pathway, step by step

  1. Build foundations. Read widely, ask questions and learn to write clearly.
  2. Choose a learning route. Complete a first degree, then explore postgraduate study in a field you love.
  3. Practise with real work. Assist with teaching, labs or research, and practise presenting your findings.
  4. Build evidence. Keep a record of research, talks and writing.
  5. Enter and keep progressing. Look for teaching and research posts and keep building your expertise.

School subjects that help

  • English Language
  • Core Mathematics
  • Social Studies

Build these subjects week by week with GuidEd's lesson plans for every class.

Skills to build

  • Research
  • Writing
  • Teaching
  • Critical thinking

A first step you can take now

Pick a question that interests you and find three reliable sources about it.
