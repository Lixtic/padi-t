🏘️ Social Worker: career pathway in Ghana

Help individuals, families and communities with social challenges and connect them to support.

Your pathway, step by step

  1. Build foundations. Build communication skills and learn about the challenges your community faces.
  2. Choose a learning route. Explore social work, sociology or community development programmes at tertiary level.
  3. Practise with real work. Complete supervised fieldwork or volunteering with community organisations.
  4. Build evidence. Keep a record of your supervised practice and what you learned.
  5. Enter and keep progressing. Look for roles in public agencies and NGOs, and keep learning the rules and standards that apply.
