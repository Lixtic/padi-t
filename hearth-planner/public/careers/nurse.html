🩹 Registered Nurse: career pathway in Ghana

Provide patient care, health education, clinical support and prevention services.

Your pathway, step by step

  1. Build foundations. Strengthen science, Mathematics, English and communication skills.
  2. Research accredited programmes. Compare recognised nursing programmes and their current WASSCE or other entry requirements.
  3. Complete professional training. Undertake the approved nursing programme with classroom and supervised clinical learning.
  4. Meet registration requirements. Complete the current Nursing and Midwifery Council of Ghana registration and licensing process.
  5. Choose a practice area. Explore community health, paediatrics, emergency care, midwifery pathways and other areas according to approved qualifications.
