Hospitality & Services · Career orientation

🎉 Event Planner: career pathway in Ghana

Organise weddings, conferences and community events so they run smoothly.

Your pathway, step by step

  1. Build foundations. Practise planning, budgeting and working with other people on school or church events.
  2. Choose a learning route. Explore hospitality, events or business programmes, or learn by working with an experienced planner.
  3. Practise with real work. Help plan events and write a checklist, budget and timetable for each.
  4. Build evidence. Keep photos, budgets and feedback from events you helped run.
  5. Enter and keep progressing. Work with a planning firm, venue or caterer, then take on your own clients.

School subjects that help

  • Business Management
  • English Language
  • Core Mathematics

Skills to build

  • Planning
  • Budgeting
  • Negotiation
  • Problem-solving

A first step you can take now

Plan a small school event on paper with a budget, a timetable and a list of tasks.
