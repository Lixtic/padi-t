📚 Professional Teacher: career pathway in Ghana

Help learners develop knowledge, skills, confidence and positive learning habits.

Your pathway, step by step

  1. Discover your teaching interests. Identify the age group and subject areas you would enjoy teaching.
  2. Build academic foundations. Develop strong subject knowledge and communication skills.
  3. Choose an accredited teacher education route. Research approved initial teacher education programmes and current admission requirements.
  4. Complete professional preparation. Undertake pedagogy, subject methods and supervised teaching practice.
  5. Meet licensing requirements. Confirm the current National Teaching Council licensing and registration requirements.
  6. Grow as an educator. Develop through mentoring, professional learning, leadership and specialist roles.
