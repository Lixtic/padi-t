⛏️ Geologist and Mining Professional: career pathway in Ghana

Study rocks and the earth to find and responsibly manage minerals and water.

Your pathway, step by step

  1. Build foundations. Strengthen science and Mathematics and learn to observe rocks, soil and landscape.
  2. Choose a learning route. Explore geology, mining or earth science programmes at tertiary level.
  3. Practise with real work. Take part in fieldwork, sampling and safety training.
  4. Build evidence. Keep field notes, maps and lab records.
  5. Enter and keep progressing. Look for roles in mining, exploration, water and environmental firms, with strong attention to safety and environmental rules.

A first step you can take now

Collect three different rocks or soils near you and describe how each looks and feels.
