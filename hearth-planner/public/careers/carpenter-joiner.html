🪚 Carpenter and Joiner: career pathway in Ghana

Make and fit furniture, doors, roofs and wooden structures.

Your pathway, step by step

  1. Build foundations. Learn to measure accurately, read simple drawings and use hand tools safely.
  2. Choose a learning route. Explore a TVET programme or apprenticeship in carpentry and joinery, and ask about assessment and certification.
  3. Practise with real work. Build joints, furniture and fittings under supervision and learn to estimate materials.
  4. Build evidence. Keep photos and records of finished work and the skills assessed.
  5. Enter and keep progressing. Work with a builder, workshop or furniture maker, and grow towards design, supervision or your own business.
