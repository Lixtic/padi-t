🖥️ ICT Support Technician: career pathway in Ghana

Set up, maintain and repair computers, networks and software for schools and organisations.

Your pathway, step by step

  1. Build foundations. Get comfortable with computers, basic networks and safe handling of equipment and data.
  2. Choose a learning route. Explore an IT or computer technology programme at TVET or tertiary level, and professional certificates.
  3. Practise with real work. Help fix and maintain computers at school or in a small business under supervision.
  4. Build evidence. Keep a log of faults diagnosed and fixed, and what you learned from each.
  5. Enter and keep progressing. Work in an IT help desk, school, business or service centre, and grow towards networking or systems roles.

  • Core Mathematics
