🗂️ Health Information and Records Officer: career pathway in Ghana

Manage patient records and health data accurately and safely.

Your pathway, step by step

  1. Build foundations. Develop accuracy with numbers and records, and learn to treat private information with care.
  2. Choose a learning route. Explore health information, records management or related programmes at TVET or tertiary level.
  3. Practise with real work. Practise with supervised data entry, filing and reporting, with strict privacy rules.
  4. Build evidence. Keep a record of training and supervised work, without sharing patient information.
  5. Enter and keep progressing. Work in clinics, hospitals and health agencies, and check current rules on health data with the relevant authority.
