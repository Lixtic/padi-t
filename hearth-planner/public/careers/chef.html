🍳 Chef and Culinary Professional: career pathway in Ghana

Prepare food safely and creatively in restaurants, hotels, catering businesses and institutions.

Your pathway, step by step

  1. Build foundations. Learn basic cooking, food safety, hygiene and measuring at home or school.
  2. Choose a learning route. Explore a catering or culinary programme at a TVET provider, hospitality institute or tertiary institution.
  3. Practise with real work. Cook for family and events, learn from experienced cooks and practise costing a meal.
  4. Build evidence. Record dishes you can prepare well, with notes on cost, hygiene and timing.
  5. Enter and keep progressing. Work in a kitchen, catering business or institution, and grow towards a specialism or your own food business.

School subjects that help

  • Home Economics
  • Core Mathematics
  • English Language

Skills to build

  • Food safety
  • Time management
  • Teamwork
  • Costing

A first step you can take now

Plan and cook one simple meal for four people and work out what it cost.
