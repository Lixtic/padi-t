🩺 Medical Doctor: career pathway in Ghana

Assess patients, diagnose illness, provide treatment and support health and wellbeing.

Your pathway, step by step

  1. Build science foundations. Develop strong foundations in Biology, Chemistry, Physics, Mathematics and English.
  2. Choose an appropriate SHS pathway. Research science programmes and confirm subject combinations accepted by the medical schools you may apply to.
  3. Meet medical school entry requirements. Check current WASSCE requirements, admissions processes, aptitude or interview requirements directly with accredited institutions.
  4. Complete medical education. Undertake the approved medical degree and clinical training required by the institution and regulator.
  5. Complete supervised practice. Meet the Medical and Dental Council's current registration and supervised practice requirements.
  6. Develop your specialty. Explore postgraduate training and continuing professional development.

A first step you can take now

Explore Biology and human anatomy, speak with a health professional and research accredited medical schools.

Medicine has competitive entry and regulated training. Confirm current requirements with the Medical and Dental Council and each university.
