💹 Economist: career pathway in Ghana

Study how people, businesses and governments use resources, and advise on decisions.

Your pathway, step by step

  1. Build foundations. Build Mathematics and reading skills, and follow how prices and jobs change around you.
  2. Choose a learning route. Explore economics, statistics or related programmes at tertiary level.
  3. Practise with real work. Practise collecting data, building simple models and explaining findings in plain language.
  4. Build evidence. Keep short reports that show your question, data and conclusion.
  5. Enter and keep progressing. Look for roles in research, government, banks and international organisations.

A first step you can take now

Track the price of three common items in your market for a month and write what you notice.
