🏗️ Civil Engineer: career pathway in Ghana

Plan, design and oversee infrastructure such as roads, bridges, buildings and water systems.

Your pathway, step by step

  1. Build STEM foundations. Develop Mathematics, Physics, problem-solving and technical drawing skills.
  2. Choose a suitable SHS or TVET route. Research science and technical programmes that prepare learners for engineering study.
  3. Study civil engineering. Compare accredited engineering degrees and other recognised technician pathways.
  4. Gain practical experience. Develop skills through supervised projects, site exposure, internships and engineering software.
  5. Meet professional standards. Check current registration and professional development expectations with the relevant engineering bodies.
  6. Progress. Grow into structural, transport, water, geotechnical or project engineering.
