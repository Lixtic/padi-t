🚀 Entrepreneur / Business Founder: career pathway in Ghana

Identify a problem, create a product or service and build a sustainable organisation.

Your pathway, step by step

  1. Notice a real problem. Observe needs in your community and identify a problem people are willing to solve.
  2. Build business foundations. Learn communication, numeracy, customer research, budgeting and basic digital skills.
  3. Test a small idea. Speak with potential customers, create a simple prototype and test demand responsibly.
  4. Learn to operate. Understand pricing, record keeping, marketing, customer service and legal responsibilities.
  5. Formalise when appropriate. Research business registration, tax and sector-specific permits with the relevant Ghanaian authorities.
  6. Grow sustainably. Improve the product, manage cash flow, build a team and explore responsible expansion.

School subjects that help

  • Core Mathematics
  • English Language
  • Business Management
  • Economics

Skills to build

  • Initiative
  • Financial literacy
  • Communication
  • Resilience

A first step you can take now

Interview three people about a problem they face and propose a small solution.

Entrepreneurship has financial risk. Start small, validate demand and seek qualified advice on legal and tax obligations.
