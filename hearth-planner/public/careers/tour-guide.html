🧳 Tour Guide and Tourism Officer: career pathway in Ghana

Welcome visitors and share the history, culture and places of Ghana.

Your pathway, step by step

  1. Build foundations. Learn the history and geography of your area and practise telling it clearly and accurately.
  2. Choose a learning route. Explore tourism and hospitality programmes at TVET or tertiary level, and learn languages that visitors use.
  3. Practise with real work. Guide a school or community visit to a local site, and ask for feedback.
  4. Build evidence. Keep a record of sites you can present well, with checked facts.
  5. Enter and keep progressing. Look for roles with tour operators, heritage sites, hotels and tourism agencies.

School subjects that help

  • History
  • Social Studies
  • English Language

Build these subjects week by week with GuidEd's lesson plans for every class.

Skills to build

  • Storytelling
  • Safety awareness
  • Languages
  • Customer care

A first step you can take now

Prepare a three-minute talk about a place near you, with facts you have checked.
