💇 Beautician and Hairstylist: career pathway in Ghana

Provide hair, skin and beauty services with care for safety and hygiene.

Your pathway, step by step

  1. Build foundations. Learn about hygiene, skin and hair care, and how to talk with customers.
  2. Choose a learning route. Explore a recognised TVET or apprenticeship programme in hairdressing or beauty therapy.
  3. Practise with real work. Practise under supervision, learn safe use of products and tools, and build a client base.
  4. Build evidence. Keep a portfolio of your work, with permission from clients.
  5. Enter and keep progressing. Work in a salon or start your own business, and keep learning new techniques and safety practices.

School subjects that help

Skills to build

  • Hygiene
  • Technique
  • Customer care
  • Business basics

A first step you can take now

Learn the hygiene steps a salon follows and practise explaining them.
