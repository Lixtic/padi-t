⚡ Electrical & Solar Technician: career pathway in Ghana

Install, inspect and maintain electrical systems and solar power equipment safely.

Your pathway, step by step

  1. Build practical foundations. Develop Mathematics, basic science, safety awareness and hands-on problem-solving.
  2. Choose a TVET or apprenticeship route. Research recognised electrical installation and renewable energy programmes.
  3. Train and practise under supervision. Learn wiring, electrical principles, solar PV components, testing and safe installation.
  4. Gain recognised certification. Check current trade testing, competency certification and licensing requirements with the relevant Ghanaian authorities.
  5. Build experience. Work with qualified practitioners and document supervised projects.
  6. Progress or start a business. Develop expertise in solar design, maintenance, energy audits or a compliant installation business.
