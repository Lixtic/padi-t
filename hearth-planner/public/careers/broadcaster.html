🎙️ Radio and TV Broadcaster: career pathway in Ghana

Present, report and produce programmes for radio, television and online audiences.

Your pathway, step by step

  1. Build foundations. Read widely, practise speaking clearly, and follow how presenters prepare their stories.
  2. Choose a learning route. Explore communication, media or journalism programmes, and look for school or campus radio opportunities.
  3. Practise with real work. Present school announcements or programmes, record yourself and listen back.
  4. Build evidence. Keep recordings and scripts of your best work.
  5. Enter and keep progressing. Look for internships at radio and TV stations, then grow into presenting, producing or reporting.

  • Writing for broadcast

A first step you can take now

Record a one-minute news summary of something that happened this week and listen for how to improve it.
