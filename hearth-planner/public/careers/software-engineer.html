💻 Software Engineer: career pathway in Ghana

Design, build and maintain software used by people and organisations.

Your pathway, step by step

  1. Build foundations. Strengthen Mathematics, English, logical reasoning and digital literacy at Basic and SHS levels.
  2. Choose a learning route. Explore Computer Science, Software Engineering, Information Technology, a recognised TVET programme or a structured coding pathway.
  3. Develop practical skills. Learn programming, web development, databases, testing, version control and secure coding.
  4. Build evidence. Create useful projects, document your work and collaborate on real-world problems.
  5. Enter the profession. Seek internships, apprenticeships, graduate roles or junior developer opportunities.
  6. Keep progressing. Grow into senior engineering, software architecture, product leadership or entrepreneurship.

Try a beginner programming project and build a small website or useful tool.
