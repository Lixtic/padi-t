Ads on GuidEd

GuidEd is free to start. The free version shows one small ad on the start screen, which helps pay for the service. GuidEd Pro has no ads.

Who sees ads

  • Visitors who are not signed in, and teachers on the free plan, see one banner on the start screen.
  • Pro teachers never see ads.

Where ads never appear

  • Inside a lesson plan, while a plan is being written, or in notes and exam questions.
  • In Word files you export, or in anything you share.
  • In sign-in, payment or account screens.

What we promise

  • One ad at a time, clearly marked as an advertisement, and kept apart from the buttons you use.
  • We never ask you to tap or click an ad, and no ad is ever shown as if it were part of GuidEd.
  • We do not give advertisers your plans, your chats, your name or your email.
  • GuidEd is a tool for teachers. It is not made for children to use, and we do not aim ads at children.

Who provides the ads

Ads come from Google AdSense. We ask Google for non-personalised ads, which means they are not based on your browsing history. Google may still use cookies or device identifiers to show ads and to count how often they are shown. You can read how Google handles this at policies.google.com/technologies/ads, and change your own ad settings at adssettings.google.com.

Prefer no ads?

GuidEd Pro removes the ad. You can also tell us if an ad looks wrong or unsuitable, using Report a bug or suggest an idea in the menu, or the contact below, and we will look into it.

More about how we handle data is on the Privacy page.
